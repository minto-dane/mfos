# IBM Z Architecture Grounding

Public-safe MFOS grounding artifacts for IBM Z architecture, partitioning, and
management-plane concepts. These cards are for mapping and divergence only; they
do not claim emulation, PR/SM compatibility, DPM compatibility, or z/OS
compatibility.

## Index

- `source-cards/z-architecture-source-cards.yml` - verified IBM source pointers and MFOS mappings.
- `concept-cards/z-architecture-concepts.yml` - MFOS concepts grounded by those pointers.
- `notes/public-safe-grounding-notes.md` - review notes, gaps, and negative tests.
